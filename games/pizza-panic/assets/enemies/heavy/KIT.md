## heavy (OpenClaw heavy)

Palette: gunmetal riveted plate armour, purple visor and purple claw crest, pauldron upper arm, fist gauntlet, armoured boots, hydraulic sledgehammer with a purple core.  Moves: hammer slam (uarm -150 to 40, 1.0s wind-up with a ground-crack telegraph, super armour during wind-up and swing, shockwave), shoulder guard (uarm -40 farm -60, blocks frontal light hits).  Behavior: Slow (0.5x walk), high HP; the hammer slam has super armour (no flinch) and a long recovery that is the punish window; can raise a guard against frontal light hits, so throws and jump kicks work best.
