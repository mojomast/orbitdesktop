# AGENT ARMEGEDDON: CLAWPOCALYPSE NOW enemy cutout rigs (iteration 5)

Generated by MR.INDIA. Same conventions as `../rig/RIG.md`: torso scaled to 68px tall (assembled ~180px; iteration 7, 2x re-slice from the source sheets), coordinates are part-local px from the image top-left, angles in degrees clockwise-positive, `_b` back limbs reuse the same image darkened (`brightness(0.6)`), same draw order and pose table (idle/walk/punch/kick/jump) as RIG.md. All enemies face **right** natively (iteration 8 audit: the art faces right, like the heroes); the game mirrors them when they face left. Files: `<type>/<type>_{head,torso,uarm,farm,thigh,shin,weapon}.png`, source sheet `<type>/sheet.jpg`, overview `contact_sheet.png`. Shins include feet/boots.

Pivots below are computed from each image: head = neck bottom centre, limbs = top centre (shoulder/elbow/hip/knee), weapon = grip (centre for wheel/shield/gauntlet). Attach points are on the parent: head at torso top centre, shoulders at torso (w*0.3 / w*0.65, 8), hips at (w*0.35 / w*0.6, h-4), farm at uarm bottom, shin at thigh bottom, weapon at farm bottom (front hand; blocker shield on back hand farm_b). Tweak by eye in the rig preview.
