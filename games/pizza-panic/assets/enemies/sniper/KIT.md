## sniper (OpenClaw sniper)

Palette: black tactical mask with a red scope eye, ghillie fringe over a black plate carrier with the purple claw, grey sleeves, black gloves, charcoal cargo pants, laced boots, long black rifle with a red scope.  Moves: aim (uarm -80 farm -10, rifle level; a red laser line telegraphs the shot for ~0.8s, then a fast tracer), reload hop back (0.6s).  Behavior: Rooftop marksman: keeps 300-450px away on high ground, paints the hero with a laser line before firing (line locks 0.25s before the shot so it can be dodged by moving off the lane), retreats when approached; low HP.
