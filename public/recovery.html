Registered-plugin recovery hold

A persistent hold prevents the server from accepting workspace changes that activate registered plugins. It is separate from disabling apps, which only changes the saved layout. Releasing hold does not re-enable apps; review and enable them separately.

The hold does not stop backends or disconnected frames, block direct access to raw /apps files, control general external browser tabs or terminals, or revoke all permissions. Cached or offline pages may render before contacting the server; this is not full safe boot.
