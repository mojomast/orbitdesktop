## 1. Power generation

| Source | Count | Rating each | Installed (peak) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Solar array wing | 4 | 1.55 kW | 6.20 kW |
| Regenerative fuel cell (primary) | 1 | 1.20 kW | 1.20 kW |
| **Installed peak generation** | | | **7.40 kW** |

**Fuel cell count reconciliation.** The manifest (EQU-007) procures **two**
regenerative fuel cells. Only **one** is counted as continuous generation. The
second unit is a rotatable spare held for the Stage-3 hot-swap, is not wired to
the daytime bus, and is **excluded** from all generation figures in this
document. Installed *continuous* generation from the fuel cell is therefore
1.20 kW, not 2.40 kW. Earlier drafts that quoted "7.40 kW installed, 2 fuel
cells" were ambiguous; this revision states the basis explicitly.

## 2. Site conditions

Site illumination factor is **70 percent**. Average solar output is therefore
6.20 kW × 0.70 = **4.34 kW**. Nights are covered by the fuel cell and the battery
bank. The battery bank (EQU-009, 5 units) is an energy buffer; it is not a
generation source and is not counted toward continuous generation.

**Peak is not continuous.** The 7.40 kW figure above is nameplate/peak installed
capacity at full illumination with the fuel cell at rating. It must not be used
as evidence that a continuous-power requirement is met. The continuous basis is
average solar plus fuel cell:

- Average continuous generation = 4.34 kW + 1.20 kW = **5.54 kW**

## 3. Demand

Per-unit continuous demand is carried in the manifest column
`unit_power_demand_kw`, and the line total is `quantity × unit_power_demand_kw`.
Manifest continuous demand total is **6.42 kW**.

- Against **peak** installed generation (7.40 kW) the margin appears to be
  **+0.98 kW**. This comparison is not valid for a continuous requirement.
- Against **average continuous** generation (5.54 kW) the full-manifest margin is
  **−0.88 kW**, a deficit of 0.88 kW.

## 4. Crewed power threshold

Crewed occupancy requires **4.7 kW continuous**, inclusive of a 0.7 kW reserve
for life-support transients.

**THIS SUPERSEDES the 4.0 kW figure quoted in DOC-04 section 2.** The higher
figure is the binding value.

Against average continuous generation (5.54 kW) the crewed critical load of
4.7 kW leaves a margin of **+0.84 kW**. The full manifest demand (6.42 kW) is
*not* satisfied continuously; only the crewed critical load is.

## 5. Cargo mass

3,400 kg per flight is a hard structural limit. Per-unit mass is carried in the
manifest column `unit_cargo_mass_kg`; line total is `quantity × unit_cargo_mass_kg`.

Total manifest mass is **7,315 kg**, so a minimum of **three flights** is
required (7,315 / 3,400 = 2.15, rounded up).

## 7. Critical path (Stage 2, excludes laboratory)

habitat × power × communications × logistics × life support

= 0.980 × 0.965 × 0.972 × 0.988 × 0.960 = **0.8719**

Against the 0.900 target this is a shortfall of **0.0281**. The product is
computed in `validate_asteria.py`; do not round it up to 0.900.

## 8. Upgraded critical path

With both Kestrel high-reliability variants (DOC-02 section 4), power becomes
0.990 and life support becomes 0.978:

= 0.980 × 0.990 × 0.972 × 0.988 × 0.978 = **0.9112**

which meets the 0.900 target. Note that a *single* upgrade is not sufficient:

- Fuel-cell variant only (power 0.990, life support 0.960) = **0.8944** — fails.
- Atmosphere variant only (power 0.965, life support 0.978) = **0.8882** — fails.

Both variants are required together, at a combined cost of **USD 184,000**.

## 9. Structural and thermal

- No single flight above 3,400 kg.
- Deployable solar arrays need an 8 m clear radius.
- Laboratory instruments require a 0.60 kW dedicated continuous feed
  (manifest EQU-015, `unit_power_demand_kw` = 0.60).

## 10. Schedule interaction with communications

The Ka-band relay terminal has a 12-month lead time from the manifest. DOC-04
section 3 asserts a different availability date. Engineering notes that if crewed
arrival is planned at month 11, the relay cannot be operational 30 days prior,
under **either** source's figure. This is a scheduling constraint, not a
generation or structural one.
