# ASTERIA LUNAR RESEARCH OUTPOST — MISSION REQUIREMENTS

Document ID: DOC-01 | Revision A | **SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DATA**
Owner: Asteria Programme Office (fictional organisation)

> Everything in this kit is invented for demonstration. The programme, its
> suppliers, its budget and every figure are synthetic. Nothing here is
> engineering, procurement or financial advice.

## 1. Purpose

Establish a six-module lunar research outpost with an initial crewed mission of
180 days, followed by expansion. The six modules are:

1. habitat
2. power
3. communications
4. laboratory
5. logistics
6. life support

## 2. Budget

Total equipment budget is **USD 2,000,000**. All monetary values are synthetic
US dollars. The budget covers equipment line items only; it excludes launch,
operations and contingency beyond the stated remaining balance.

## 3. Deployment

Deployment occurs in three stages:

- **Stage 1** — uncrewed surface build.
- **Stage 2** — crewed arrival and occupancy.
- **Stage 3** — laboratory and logistics expansion.

Crewed arrival must occur no earlier than month 11 and no later than month 18.
The laboratory must be operational no later than month 28.

## 4. Cargo

Each cargo lander flight is certified for **3,400 kg of landed mass**. No single
flight may exceed this limit. Total manifest mass determines the minimum number
of flights.

## 5. Reliability

Stage 2 critical-path system reliability shall be **at least 0.900** over five
years. "Critical path" for Stage 2 is defined in DOC-03 section 7 and excludes
the laboratory.

## 6. Power

Crewed occupancy requires continuous power at or above the threshold stated in
DOC-03 section 4. DOC-03 is the engineering authority. Where DOC-04 states a
different threshold, **DOC-03 takes precedence**.

## 7. Communications

The Ka-band relay terminal must be ordered at programme start (month 0) and must
be operational no later than 30 days before crewed arrival.

## 8. Procurement

All line items with a lead time of 12 months or more must be ordered at programme
start (month 0).

## 9. Precedence between source documents

- **DOC-03** (Engineering Constraints) takes precedence on all physical limits,
  thresholds and reliability figures.
- **DOC-02** (Supplier Assessment) is commercial assessment only; it carries no
  engineering authority.
- **DOC-04** (Operations Memo) is an informal operations memo. It records
  operational assumptions and preferences, not binding limits.
