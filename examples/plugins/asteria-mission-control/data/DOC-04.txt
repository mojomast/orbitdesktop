# OPERATIONS MEMO — SURFACE INTENT AND ASSUMPTIONS

From: Kayla Renn, Operations Lead (fictional)
To: Asteria Programme Office
**SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DATA**

> Informal internal memo. Operational preference and intent. **Not an engineering
> authority.** Where this memo differs from DOC-03, DOC-03 governs.

## 1. Intent

We prefer to prove power and communications on the surface before we commit
crew. I would rather the crew arrive to a quiet, already-breathing base than to a
base whose critical systems are landing in the same window as the crew.

## 2. Power assumption

Operations is **working to a crewed continuous power threshold of 4.0 kW**.
Engineering has been asked to review this figure. We understand the review may
raise it.

## 3. Communications

The relay terminal must be **operational 30 days before crewed arrival** so that
the blackout rule is never breached.

Relay terminal delivery is **not expected before month 14**. Plan comms closeout
on that basis. (Commercial has separately quoted a 12-month lead time; I am
planning conservatively against month 14.)

## 4. Logistics

One surface rover is sufficient for the first 180 days. Rover battery packs are
treated as consumables and we expect to cycle them. We have not yet confirmed a
delivery lead time for the spare battery packs.

## 5. Budget view

We expect the manifest to total approximately 1.95 million, leaving roughly
50,000 of contingency. **No allowance has been made for reliability upgrades.**
If both upgrades are wanted, something has to come out of the manifest.

## 6. Data hygiene

Our first manifest export appears to include a duplicated communications line
item. Finance should reconcile the manifest before the budget gate. Operations
has no view on which row is correct.

## 7. Crewed arrival preference

Operations would prefer crewed arrival closer to month 14 than to month 11,
because a month-11 arrival leaves no margin for the relay closeout. This is a
preference, not a requirement; DOC-01 section 3 sets the binding window of
months 11–18.
