## 1. Scope

Four qualified suppliers: Selene Systems, Kestrel Aerospace, Meridian Robotics,
and Apollon Dynamics. This assessment covers price basis, lead times and offered
reliability variants.

## 2. Spend profile

Reconciled manifest, total **USD 1,953,000** (24 distinct line items). Figures
exclude the suspected duplicate communications row; see section 7.

| Supplier | Reconciled spend (USD) | Share |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Kestrel Aerospace | 612,000 | 31.3% |
| Selene Systems | 547,000 | 28.0% |
| Meridian Robotics | 489,000 | 25.0% |
| Apollon Dynamics | 305,000 | 15.6% |

No supplier exceeds the **35%** concentration threshold. Kestrel Aerospace is
nevertheless the largest single source of spend.

## 3. Lead times and commitments

Kestrel Aerospace delivers the **Ka-band relay terminal on a 12-month lead
time**. Ordered at month 0, delivery lands at **month 12**, and we assess the
terminal as installable and available for a Stage-1 closeout at month 12.

The regenerative fuel cell is on a **15-month** lead time.

The optical communications backup terminal is on a **16-month** lead time — the
longest item in the manifest — and must be ordered at month 0 under DOC-01
section 8.

## 4. Reliability variants offered

| Variant | Supplier | Uplift cost (USD) | Uplift mass (kg) |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Regenerative fuel cell, high-reliability variant | Kestrel Aerospace | 120,000 | 50 |
| Atmosphere revitalisation unit, high-reliability variant | Kestrel Aerospace | 64,000 | 30 |

Combined uplift: **USD 184,000** and **80 kg**.

We recommend both variants for any strategy targeting crewed arrival before
month 16.

## 5. Risk note

Kestrel Aerospace holds **both** recommended reliability variants. That creates a
single-source dependency on the programme's two highest-consequence upgrades.

## 6. Commercial view of the schedule

Purchase order release at month 0 lets the relay terminal land at month 12. We
consider a Stage 1 closeout at month 12 achievable on that basis. This is a
commercial lead-time statement, not a schedule authority.

## 7. Manifest reconciliation note

The first manifest export contained a duplicated communications line item.
Reconciled totals in this document exclude that row. The raw export total is
**USD 2,005,000** and the reconciled total is **USD 1,953,000**. Finance should
reconcile the manifest before the budget gate.
